Simple English Wiktionary (extract)
https://simple.wiktionary.org

Source of this file: the wiktextract extract of the Simple English Wiktionary published by kaikki.org
(https://kaikki.org/dictionary/downloads/simple/simple-extract.jsonl.gz, data from the October 2026
dump), converted and reduced by tools/convert_simplewikt.py in the Wordhoard repository: English
entries only; per word at most eight senses with their part of speech, first definition, first example,
synonyms and antonyms; senses marked old or rare removed; specialised senses moved after general ones.
