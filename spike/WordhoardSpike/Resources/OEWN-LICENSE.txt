oewn-2024.json in this folder is a trimmed derivative of the release (definitions,
synonyms, antonyms and sense order only; at most three senses per part of speech),
produced by spike/tools/convert_oewn.py. The XML examples, pronunciations and all
other relations were removed. No other changes were made to the content.
